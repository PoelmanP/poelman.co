Privacy policy

The Google OAuth application registered under this domain is a private tool used only by Peter Poelman to work with his own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar and Contacts).

  • It is not offered to the public and is used only with accounts owned by Peter Poelman.
  • Data read from Google is processed only on Peter Poelman's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time via myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
